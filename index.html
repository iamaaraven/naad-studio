Publish & monetize

Naad does not upload for you or pay you — it prepares assets and guides. You need your own accounts.

YouTube
Create a channel, upload lyric/WebM, enable monetization when eligible (watch time + subscribers rules).
Instagram / Reels
Post vertical clip; use suggested hashtags; Professional account for insights.
Spotify for Artists
Claim profile after a distributor delivers your track — Naad cannot push to Spotify directly.
DistroKid / TuneCore
Pay their fees, upload WAV/MP3 + cover art yourself. Follow their content & rights rules.
